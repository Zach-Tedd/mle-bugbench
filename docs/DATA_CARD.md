# Data card

- **Source and licence:** where it came from, URL, licence, version / date accessed
- **Size and classes:** rows or items per class, before and after cleaning
- **Collection and labelling:** who labelled it, guidelines, agreement (e.g. Cohen's kappa)
- **Cleaning:** duplicates removed, label errors fixed, with counts
- **Splits:** method (stratified / grouped by source / by time) and why
- **Known biases and gaps**
- **Personal or sensitive data:** none, or how it was removed
